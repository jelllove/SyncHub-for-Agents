SyncHub for Agents keeps your AI workspace portable across devices.
If you use Claude, Copilot, and Gemini, losing config and session context across machines is a daily pain.
SyncHub solves that with a secure private-repo relay, scheduled sync, and conflict-safe merges.
You choose exactly what to sync: sessions, safe config, instructions, skills, and shared resources.
Secrets and machine-local credentials stay local by design.
The dashboard shows progress in real time, while settings let you control cadence, retention, and included agents.
When conflicts happen, SyncHub provides clear local, remote, and merged choices with batch apply.
The result is simple: less setup time, fewer surprises, and consistent agent behavior everywhere.
